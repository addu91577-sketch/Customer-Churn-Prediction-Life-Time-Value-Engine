# Customer Churn Business Insights

## 1. Overall Churn
- Total customers: 7043
- Churned customers: 1869
- Overall churn rate: 26.54%

## 2. Contract
- Month-to-month contracts exhibit a significantly higher churn rate (~42.7%) compared to One-year (~11.3%) and Two-year (~2.8%) contracts.

## 3. Tenure
- Newer customers (tenure < 12 months) show the highest concentration of churn. Retention rates stabilize significantly after 24 months.

## 4. Monthly Charges
- Higher monthly charges correlate with increased churn. Customers paying over $70/month show higher churn proportions than lower-tier subscribers.

## 5. Internet Service
- Fiber optic subscribers exhibit a higher churn rate (~41.9%) than DSL subscribers (~19.0%) or those without internet service.

## 6. Payment Method
- Customers using Electronic Check show an observed churn rate of ~45.3%, which is higher than automated payment methods (Bank transfer, Credit card).

## 7. Customer Characteristics
- Single customers (No Partner) and those without Dependents present higher churn rates compared to customers with families.
- Senior citizens present a higher churn percentage (~41.7%) relative to non-senior customers (~23.6%).

---

## Business Recommendations

1. **Incentivize Long-Term Contracts:** Offer targeted discounts or value-added perks to migrate Month-to-month users to 1-Year or 2-Year plans.
2. **Improve Onboarding for High-Risk Service Tiers:** Review pricing and service reliability for Fiber Optic plans to address customer drop-offs within the first 12 months.
3. **Promote Automated Payment Options:** Encourage shifting from Electronic Checks to Auto-Pay (Credit Card/Bank Transfer) through setup bonuses.
